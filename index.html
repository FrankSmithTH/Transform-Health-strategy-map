Our Vision

🌍

Global Coordination

Transform Health works across national, regional, and global levels simultaneously to create enabling environments for digital health transformation worldwide.

📊

Evidence-Based Strategy

A comprehensive roadmap with 5 core objectives: policy & law, data governance, investment reform, civil society, and coalition building.

🤝

Multi-Stakeholder Coalition

230+ partners spanning governments, civil society, private sector, and international organizations working toward shared outcomes.

Three Levels of Engagement

Transform Health operates simultaneously at national, regional, and global levels to ensure policy reform flows from the bottom up and normative standards cascade from the top down.

🌐 Global

Setting international norms through WHO and World Health Assembly, establishing frameworks that Member States commit to achieving.

🌏 Regional

Developing Africa Continental and Asia Regional Health Data Governance Frameworks that guide national implementation while respecting regional context.

🏛️ National

Supporting 9 national coalitions to develop enabling legislation, data governance reforms, and investment accountability mechanisms with full civil society participation.

Five Core Objectives

Objective 1: Policy & Law

Develop and enact comprehensive digital health legislation that ensures rights-based governance, civil society participation, and accountability mechanisms in all 9 national coalition countries.

Objective 2: Data Governance

Create Africa Continental and Asia Regional Health Data Governance Frameworks, with 23 countries aligning national legislation to these regional standards by 2030.

Objective 3: Investment

Establish a digital health investment taxonomy adopted by 20 countries and 6 multilateral agencies, integrated into global financial tracking systems (OECD DAC, WHO SHA).

Objective 4: Civil Society

Strengthen meaningful participation of youth and civil society in governance and accountability at national and global levels across all objectives.

Objective 5: Coalition Building

Activate 10+ goal-based cluster groups and maintain 70% active engagement of 230+ coalition partners to drive collective action toward all objectives by 2030.
