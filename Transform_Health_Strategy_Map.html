Transform Health Coalition · Strategy Map 2027–2030

Fondation Botnar Grant Application  |  National · Regional · Global Engagement

Transform Health works at three levels simultaneously. National coalitions build enabling environments — laws, regulations, investment accountability, workforce development and civil society engagement. Regional institutions develop continental standards and provide technical support. Global actors align international norms and financing. By 2030, the coalition will have created the conditions for digital health to scale equitably across 30+ countries, serving over 1 billion people.
